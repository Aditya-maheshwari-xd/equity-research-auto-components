## The call: Underweight, fair value ₹1,564 vs price ₹2,406

| Scenario | 10-yr revenue CAGR | Steady EBITDA margin | Value / share | vs price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bear | 7.8% | 12.5% | ₹1,001 | −58% |
| **Base** | **11.3%** | **13.8%** | **₹1,564** | **−35%** |
| Bull | 13.9% | 14.8% | ₹2,138 | −11% |

WACC 11.2% (INR risk-free 5.3% = 10Y G-sec 7.18% less India default spread 1.87%; beta 0.87; India ERP 7.08% from Damodaran), terminal growth 5%, mid-year discounting.

**Why underweight on a good business.** A reverse DCF shows that today's price needs one of the following to hold, with everything else at base case:
- ~18% revenue CAGR for ten years (the company did 11% over the last ten)
- a 9.2% WACC
- a 17.4% EBITDA margin every year (FY26: 13.2%)

Two things point the wrong way on top of that. Standalone margins fell from 13.0% to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, and free cash flow was only 58% of net profit in FY26.

**The main counter-argument**, covered in the deck, is relative valuation. At 35x trailing P/E, Endurance is the cheapest quality name in a peer group trading at around 60x. The DCF says that makes it cheaper than its peers, not cheap.

## How the model works

```
Cover         summary of outputs
Assumptions   scenario switch (C4: 1/2/3) + every driver, each with a written rationale
DCF           revenue → EBITDA → NOPAT → FCFF, terminal value, equity bridge, per-share value
Sensitivity   WACC × terminal growth grid, exit-multiple cross-check, beta × ERP
Comps         listed peer multiples
WACC          cost of capital build-up with sources
Historicals   FY22–FY26 financials and ratios
```

- Blue cells are inputs and each one cites its source. Black cells are formulas, and green cells link to other sheets.
- The equity bridge deducts debt including leases, plus the expected cost of buying the remaining 32% of Stöferle. That cost is implied from the price paid for 8% in Q1 FY27. The bridge also rolls value forward from 31 Mar 2026 to the price date at the cost of equity, net of the dividend paid.
- The workbook is generated by `dcf/build_dcf.py` and recalculated in LibreOffice with zero formula errors. The scenario values above were checked against an independent Python re-implementation of the model, and they match.

## Limitations

- Historical EBITDA follows the S&P definition, which excludes other income, so it is lower than the company-reported ₹2,090 Cr.
- Peer prices for Gabriel India and Bharat Forge are from 5 Oct 2026, eleven days older than the rest.
- The beta comes from a single data provider and the measurement window isn't disclosed, so the Sensitivity tab flexes it.
- Minority interest at Stöferle is an estimate built from the stake purchase price.
